Frequently Asked Questions

What is PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana?

It is a central government scheme that gives subsidy for rooftop solar on homes so that families can cut their electricity bills.

How much central subsidy do I get for 3 kW and above?

The central subsidy is a fixed ₹78,000 for systems of 3 kW and above in Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh.

Does Gujarat give an additional subsidy?

Yes. Gujarat adds a state subsidy for 1 kW and 2 kW systems, with a higher amount for the SC/ST category. See the calculator on this page.

Where do I apply?

Register and apply on the official PM Surya Ghar portal, then follow DISCOM approval, installation by a registered vendor, net metering and inspection.
