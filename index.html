    Comparison: Lead Acid vs Lithium

    ParameterLead Acid (12V – 160Ah)LFP (12.8V – 100Ah)
    Battery ChemistryTubular Lead-Acid▲LiFePO₄ (Lithium Iron Phosphate) — stable, safe chemistry
    Usable Capacity~80–90 Ah (50–60% DoD)▲~95–100 Ah (up to 97% DoD)
    Cycle Life (@80% DoD)~600–800 cycles▲3000+ cycles
    Charging Time12–15 hours▲3–4 hours (30A charger)
    Weight~55–60 kg▲~12–13 kg
    MaintenanceRequires periodic water topping & service▲Maintenance-free
    Safety FeaturesRisk of acid leakage & gas emission▲In-built BMS, no acid leakage
    Total Cost of OwnershipHigher over lifecycle due to replacements▲Lower over lifecycle due to long life
    ▲ Green arrow = Lithium-Ion is better than Lead Acid on this parameter.

    Why Lithium-Ion over Lead Acid in ERK

    ParameterLead Acid BatteryLithium-Ion Battery
    WeightHeavy — 4 batteries required per e-rickshaw, total 120 kg (35 kg × 4)▲Light — 1 battery required, total weight 42 kg
    Mileage EfficiencyAffected by the heavy weight of the 4-battery set▲Better mileage efficiency from the lighter battery
    Charging Time~10–12 hours▲~4.0–4.5 hours
    Warranty12–15 months on average▲3 years / 36 months
    Water Top-upRequires de-mineralised water top-up every 2–3 weeks▲No water top-up required
    Spillage RiskDry battery and acid spillage can damage the vehicle body and chassis▲No dry battery or acid spillage — body and chassis stay safe
    Self-DischargeFaster self-discharge▲Lower self-discharge — retains charge longer when idle
    ▲ Green arrow = Lithium-Ion is better than Lead Acid on this parameter.
