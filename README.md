# VION Smart Energy Hub

Free online calculator for VION lithium batteries, inverters, solar and E-Rickshaw packs, built for Indian conditions.

**Open it:** https://vion-smart-energy.github.io/

## What it does
- **Home Inverter:** pick 1 to 4 BHK or your appliances and get the right inverter, VION battery and backup hours.
- **E-Rickshaw:** right 51.2 V / 64 V lithium pack and charger, range per charge, cost per km, profit.
- **Solar (On-Grid, Hybrid, Off-Grid):** system size, panels, monthly saving, with state and district sun hours.
- **PM Surya Ghar:** subsidy guide for Gujarat and Madhya Pradesh.
- Customer View and Distributor View, WhatsApp share, shareable result link, Gujarati proposal (PDF) with QR code.
- English, Hindi and Gujarati. Works on phone and laptop, and can be added to the home screen.

## Files
- `index.html` - the whole app in one file.
- `manifest.webmanifest`, `sw.js`, `icon-192.png`, `icon-512.png` - make it installable and usable offline.
